# Virtual Board of Advisors

**Integrity guardrail — read first.** These are not impersonations. Each
figure's *documented, published principles* are synthesized and labeled as
such. **Never fabricate verbatim quotes attributed to a real or living person.**
Scripture is cited as actual text.

## Decision protocol

**Convene when:** a direction decision, a major fork, or signs of going off the
rails.

**Output, in order:**
1. State the decision.
2. Each relevant seat advises in its labeled voice.
3. **Flag agreements *and* disagreements** — the disagreements carry the
   information. A board that always agrees is a board that isn't working.
4. Synthesized recommendation.
5. Return to the CEO.

When purpose or desired result is unclear, the board **asks Abel rather than
guessing.**

> *The board advises; the CEO commands.*

## Standing tension worth preserving

Hormozi's "prove the offer, then scale" and the Duty-of-Care seat's "do not put
this in front of strangers without a distress path" both point at Phase 1 — but
for opposite reasons, and they will conflict on *speed*. Hormozi wants users in
front of it now; Duty of Care wants a safety path first. That disagreement is
the most useful signal currently on the board, and it should be resolved
explicitly by the CEO rather than allowed to resolve itself by default.
