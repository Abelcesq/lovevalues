# Project Purpose

## What it is

A faith-inclusive, values-first AI relationship confidant.

## The intention

To replace appearance-first dating with values-first discernment.

Physical appearance has near-zero correlation with core values, yet roughly
99% of dating products lead with the photograph. Most people cannot name their
own values, let alone a partner's. This tool gives them that clarity — as an
offering of love to single people.

## The crown jewel

A validated four-module method: **Values → Roots → Patterns → Habits.**

Proven deeply on one founding case. That is a strong signal and an unproven
product. Both of those are true at the same time, and the second one is the
reason Phase 1 exists.

## Governing purpose of the method

To hold up a **mirror**. It does not deliver verdicts. It reflects what the
current answers suggest, asks the user whether it resonates, and — with that
clarity — helps them:

1. Find a partner who can truly *see* and accept them, because that partner is
   also clear and can hold space; and
2. Jointly, with mutual clarity on each other's values, habits, and tendencies,
   **build the relationship they consciously agree to.**

This assumes complete honesty and candor from each person. The mirror is only
as true as the input — and the product says so out loud rather than hiding the
dependency.

## The realism check, without sugar-coating

The full end goal — a scalable mobile app serving faith-inclusive singles
worldwide — is multi-year. What is realistic *now* is to prove the method
survives contact with a stranger: that a person who is not the founder, guided
only by software, reaches clarity worth paying for.

- **Phase 0** — lock spec and decisions
- **Phase 1** — MVP (web first, then app) tested on 10–25 real people who pay
- **Phase 2** — full app, match feature, infrastructure
- **Phase 3** — scale

## Honest inventory of gaps

No second user. No monetization test. No data/privacy architecture. No legal
wrapper beyond disclosure text. These are not reasons to stop; they are the
actual work.
